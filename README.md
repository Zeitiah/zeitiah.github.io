<p><b>Cryptids</b> are <a href="/wiki/Animal" title="Animal">animals</a> or other beings whose present existence is disputed or unsubstantiated by <a href="/wiki/Science" title="Science">science</a>. <a href="/wiki/Cryptozoology" title="Cryptozoology">Cryptozoology</a>, the study of cryptids, is a <a href="/wiki/Pseudoscience" title="Pseudoscience">pseudoscience</a> claiming that such beings may exist somewhere in the wild; it has been widely critiqued by scientists.<sup id="cite_ref-MULLIS-2021-185b_1-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-MULLIS-2021-185b-1"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>1<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-LOXTON-PROTHERO-2013:_332,_320_2-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-LOXTON-PROTHERO-2013:_332,_320-2"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>2<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-CHURCH-2009-251-252_3-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-CHURCH-2009-251-252-3"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup><sup id="cite_ref-ROESCH-MOORE-2002-71-78_4-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-ROESCH-MOORE-2002-71-78-4"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> The subculture is regularly criticized for reliance on anecdotal information<sup id="cite_ref-SHERMER-2003_5-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-SHERMER-2003-5"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and because in the course of investigating animals that most scientists believe are unlikely to have existed, cryptozoologists do not follow the <a href="/wiki/Scientific_method" title="Scientific method">scientific method</a>.<sup id="cite_ref-DASH-2000_6-0" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-DASH-2000-6"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> Many scientists have criticized the plausibility of cryptids due to lack of physical evidence,<sup id="cite_ref-7" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-7"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> likely misidentifications<sup id="cite_ref-8" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-8"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> and misinterpretation of stories from folklore.<sup id="cite_ref-9" class="reference"><a href="#cite_note-9"><span class="cite-bracket">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket">]</span></a></sup> While <a href="/wiki/Biologist" title="Biologist">biologists</a> regularly identify new species following established <a href="/wiki/Scientific_methodology" class="mw-redirect" title="Scientific methodology">scientific methodology</a>, cryptozoologists focus on entities mentioned in the <a href="/wiki/Folklore" title="Folklore">folklore</a> record and rumor.
